Privacy

What this site collects, which is very little.
1 min · Muhammad Hassan Raza

This is a personal site. It doesn’t sell anything, run ads or keep a mailing list, so there isn’t much to say, but here’s all of it.

Analytics. Page views are counted with Umami, which doesn’t use cookies or track you across sites. I see which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from, never who you are.

Comments. Posts use Giscus, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions. If you comment, you do it with your GitHub account under GitHub’s privacy terms. If you don’t, Giscus loads but learns nothing about you.

Booking a call. The booking link goes to Cal.com. Whatever you enter there is handled by them and shared with me so the meeting can happen.

Email. If you write to hi@mhassan.dev, I keep the conversation like any other email and don’t share it.

That’s it. No cookies of my own, no tracking pixels, no data sold or passed on. Questions go to the same address.
