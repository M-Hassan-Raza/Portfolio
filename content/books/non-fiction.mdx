### <span className="book-subtitle">Thinking, Fast and Slow</span>
<div className="book-cover">
  <AsciiCover asset="thinking-fast-slow" alt="Thinking, Fast and Slow Cover" variant="portrait" />
</div>
<p className="author">Author: Daniel Kahneman</p>
<blockquote className="review">
A Nobel laureate explaining why humans are gloriously irrational. Introduces <strong>System 1 and System 2 thinking</strong>—the fast, intuitive brain vs. the slow, analytical one. </blockquote>
