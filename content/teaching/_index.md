I taught labs at FAST-NUCES from 2024 to 2025, in Operating Systems, Software Construction and Development, and Web Engineering. Before that I was a teaching assistant for two years, for Software Engineering, Mobile Development, Information Security and Software Project Management.

The best moments were never in the slides. They were when someone's clean solution stopped working and they had to trace what actually happened instead of guessing. Most of what I tried to teach lives in that gap.

## How I like to teach

- Start from a real system, not a toy that can't break.
- Make people debug before they get the answer.
- Talk about tradeoffs, because the textbook answer is usually one of several.
- Keep explanations plain, and assume edge cases count.
